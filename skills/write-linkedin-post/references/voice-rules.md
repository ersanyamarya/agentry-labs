# Voice rules

Apply these while drafting (step 4) and again in the editing pass (step 5) when `humanize-and-unslop` is not available.

## Tone

- Bold, product-focused storytelling in the spirit of minimalist launch writing. Do not imitate any named person's voice.
- Short, varied sentences with a natural spoken rhythm. Restraint over volume.
- Focus on the reader's experience and outcome, not a list of specifications.
- Use strong words only when the facts earn them. A modest update stays modest.
- Keep concrete claims and useful personality. Preserve lines that already sound human.

## Cut on sight

- Em dashes, and spaced en dashes used as em dashes. Use a period, comma, or colon.
- Stock openers: "thrilled to introduce", "excited to share", "I'm proud to announce", "big news", "let that sink in".
- Corporate jargon: leverage, synergy, game-changer, unlock, supercharge, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, in today's fast-paced world.
- Binary "not X, but Y" and "it's not just X, it's Y" constructions.
- Faux-insight setups: "Here's the thing", "The truth is", "Here's what nobody tells you".
- Throat-clearing and recap endings: "In summary", "At the end of the day", "So, what does this mean?".
- Repeated sentence templates (three sentences in a row starting the same way).
- Fake drama, invented stakes, rhetorical question chains.
- Title Case Headings. Use sentence case except for proper names and acronyms.

## Hashtags

- Two to four in total.
- Each one replaces a natural word inside a complete body sentence or bullet: "Keep full #control over where your data goes."
- At most one per sentence or bullet.
- Never on its own line, never appended after a sentence's final punctuation, never in a block, never in the call to action.
- If removing the `#` leaves the sentence ungrammatical, rewrite the sentence.

## Facts

- Base every claim on the supplied material or what the user confirmed.
- Never invent metrics, testimonials, customer names, capabilities, dates, or privacy guarantees.
- When a fact would strengthen the post but is missing, leave it out rather than guess.
